Analyze candidate fit for vacancy and return strategy as JSON:
{
  "positioning": "string",
  "fit_score": 0.75,
  "highlight_job_ids": [1, 2],
  "downplay_job_ids": [],
  "skills_to_highlight": ["string"],
  "skills_to_soften": ["string"],
  "gaps": ["string"],
  "resume_variants": ["string"],
  "recommendations_short": "string"
}

Context:
- Candidate has {experience_years} years experience
- Target role: {target_role}
- Candidate skills: {candidate_skills}
- Vacancy role: {vacancy_role}
- Vacancy seniority: {vacancy_seniority}
- Must-have skills: {must_have_skills}
- Nice-to-have: {nice_to_have_skills}
- Jobs in profile: {job_count}

Rules:
- fit_score: 0.0-1.0 based on skill overlap
- positioning: 1-2 sentence summary
- highlight_job_ids: which jobs most relevant
- gaps: missing must-have skills
- recommendations_short: 1-2 sentences actionable advice
- Never return empty arrays without good reason
