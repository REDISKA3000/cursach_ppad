Extraction rules:
- Extract only facts and requirements present in the vacancy text.
- Do not hallucinate requirements, tools, responsibilities, benefits, company facts, or seniority.
- ROLE must be a clean job title, not salary, schedule, location, or employment type.
- SENIORITY must be one of: Junior, Middle, Senior, Lead, Manager. Leave blank if unclear.
- INDUSTRY should be short and normalized, for example: Banking, FinTech, Payments, Retail / E-commerce, Software / SaaS, Marketing, Telecom, Consulting. Leave blank if unclear.
- MUST_HAVE = real hiring filters: core hard skills, core tools/stack, mandatory relevant experience, mandatory domain experience, mandatory role experience, or mandatory profile education.
- NICE_TO_HAVE = only explicit preferred/plus/advantage/bonus requirements, secondary tools, optional stack, or non-mandatory experience.
- Do not overfill MUST_HAVE with generic low-signal traits when stronger requirements exist.
- Do not put generic phrases like "уверенное пользование компьютером", "коммуникабельность", "стрессоустойчивость", "аккуратность", "готовность работать в команде" into MUST_HAVE unless the vacancy has almost no other requirements.
- Keep mandatory experience requirements, for example "опыт от 1 года", "опыт в банках/МФО", "опыт в роли риск-аналитика", in MUST_HAVE.
- If a line says "бонусом", "будет плюсом", "желательно", "advantage", put those items in NICE_TO_HAVE even if they are tools.
- RESPONSIBILITIES = what the person will do, as short factual bullet lines.
- ATS_KEYWORDS = concise role-relevant keywords from role, skills, requirements, and responsibilities. Use semicolon-separated terms.
- WARNINGS = only real extraction uncertainty, not generic disclaimers.

HH-like / Russian vacancy rules:
- Treat sections like "Требования", "Наши пожелания", "Что важно для нас", "ТЫ ТОЧНО СПРАВИШЬСЯ, ЕСЛИ" as requirements.
- Treat sections like "Будет плюсом", "Будет преимуществом", "Желательно" as nice-to-have.
- Treat sections like "Чем предстоит заниматься", "Задачи", "Обязанности", "Ключевые задачи" as responsibilities.
- Treat sections like "Что мы предлагаем", "Мы предлагаем", "Условия" as benefits/perks; do not extract perks into must-have.
- "Опыт работы: 1–3 года" may imply Middle.
- "Опыт работы: 3–6 лет" may imply Senior depending on role context.
- If seniority is still uncertain, leave [SENIORITY] blank and add a warning.
