Rules:
- Use only candidate facts from the context.
- Never invent companies, dates, positions, tools, education, languages, metrics, or achievements.
- If a gap exists, do not fake coverage.
- Treat Strategy as writing guidance, not as a source of new facts.
- Conservative domain grounding:
  - If a vacancy domain is explicitly confirmed by candidate jobs/company_type/responsibilities, you may state it directly.
  - If candidate evidence only partially supports the vacancy domain, use cautious wording like "опыт в смежном контуре", "релевантный опыт в аналитике", "частично применимый опыт".
  - If the domain is not confirmed in Candidate facts, do not present it as candidate experience.
- Separate facts from positioning:
  - KEY_SKILLS must contain only confirmed candidate skills, tools, methods, and domain methods.
  - SUMMARY may use positioning and priority themes as framing, but must not convert them into invented hard skills.
  - priority_themes and recommendations_short are guidance only; do not copy them as facts if they are not grounded in Candidate facts.
- Confidence-aware writing:
  - If strategy_confidence is not high, or major gaps exist, avoid overclaiming and use more careful phrasing.
  - Do not write "полностью соответствует", "эксперт в", or similar strong claims unless directly supported by Candidate facts.
- Use the strategy as the writing plan: positioning, highlighted jobs, achievement highlights, priority themes, skills, softened skills, gaps, variants, and recommendations.
- Summary must be shaped by strategy.positioning, priority_themes, and recommendations_short, but stay grounded in Candidate facts.
- SUMMARY is only a short positioning frame: maximum 2-3 sentences and maximum 40 words.
- SUMMARY is framing only, not the main proof block.
- If a theme is better demonstrated through highlighted job bullets, do not over-explain it in SUMMARY.
- Keep selling proof in RELEVANT_EXPERIENCE, not in SUMMARY.
- If there is a tradeoff between a longer summary and stronger experience bullets, keep SUMMARY shorter and make RELEVANT_EXPERIENCE stronger.
- RELEVANT_EXPERIENCE is the main selling block of the resume.
- Relevant experience should prioritize highlighted jobs and use achievement_highlights when grounded in the corresponding job.
- Highlighted jobs must feel stronger, more outcome-oriented, and more strategically relevant than additional experience.
- Bullets for highlighted jobs should explain why the candidate fits the vacancy through real facts, not through invented claims.
- For each highlighted job, write 2-4 bullets maximum.
- Prefer fewer, stronger bullets over many weaker bullets.
- Bullet priority order for highlighted jobs:
  1. grounded achievement;
  2. grounded implementation / automation / integration work;
  3. grounded analytical responsibility with clear business meaning;
  4. generic duty-like responsibility only if stronger grounded facts are absent.
- Downplayed jobs should be shortened and moved to additional experience when possible.
- For each additional/downplayed job, write 1 short bullet maximum when possible.
- Additional experience should be concise and should not overpower the target positioning.
- Achievement highlights should appear in the final text when they are grounded in candidate facts.
- Priority themes should appear in the summary and/or relevant experience.
- Skills to soften should not be prominent unless they are clearly useful for the vacancy.
- KEY_SKILLS must be vacancy-tailored, not a dump of all candidate skills.
- KEY_SKILLS must contain 6-10 items maximum.
- KEY_SKILLS priority: confirmed strategy.skills_to_highlight, confirmed vacancy core requirements, skills supported by highlighted jobs.
- Prefer concrete tools, methods, and domain-relevant analytical methods.
- Avoid broad or vague skill phrases if more specific grounded skills exist.
- If choosing between abstract and concrete skill wording, choose the concrete grounded wording.
- Never put unconfirmed vacancy requirements, gaps, or priority_themes into KEY_SKILLS.
- Bullet ranking rules:
  - choose bullets by factual grounding first;
  - then vacancy relevance;
  - then strategic distinctiveness;
  - avoid repeating the same idea across multiple jobs;
  - prefer measured outcomes only when the metric is present in Candidate facts.
- Bullet style rules:
  - start bullets with strong factual action verbs when the source fact allows it;
  - prefer bullets that show ownership, implementation, analytical depth, or business value;
  - avoid weak duty-like openings such as "участвовал", "занимался", "работал над", "выполнял" when the same fact supports a stronger honest verb;
  - prefer factual verbs like "разработал", "внедрил", "настроил", "автоматизировал", "сформировал", "анализировал", "подготовил", "интегрировал";
  - each bullet should express one strong idea.
- Preferred bullet structure when possible:
  - action;
  - object/process;
  - method/context;
  - why it mattered or what it supported.
- It is allowed to make the same fact denser and clearer, but forbidden to decorate it into an invented achievement.
- If two adjacent source facts form one stronger accomplishment, combine them into one dense bullet instead of splitting them into weak fragments.
- Avoid weak side-domain skills if they do not support the target vacancy.
- Keep the resume compact, factual, and easy to scan.
- Do not output technical markers in the final sections: no "### Job 1", no "Company:", no "Period:".
- Job heading format must be human-readable: "Position — Company", then period on the next line, then bullets.
- Each job must be a separate complete block: heading, period, then only bullets for that exact job.
- Start the next job block with its heading before writing any bullets for that next job.
- Do not merge bullets from different jobs into one block and do not render period as a bullet.
- If a section is empty, keep the header and leave it blank.
